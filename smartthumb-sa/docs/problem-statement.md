# Problem Statement

Small businesses in South Africa often use smartphone photographs to advertise products through WhatsApp Business, social media and online catalogues. Owners with limited time, technical skills or resources may resize images and organise product photos by hand, which takes time and leaves catalogues inconsistent and hard to search.

SmartThumb SA automatically creates smaller thumbnails when a product photo is uploaded and suggests visible-object labels for the owner to review.

**Validation status:** this is a hypothesis, not a confirmed finding. Interview 3-5 sellers before presenting it as confirmed.

## Users and stakeholders

- Primary: home-based and informal clothing or accessory sellers; small businesses selling handbags, shoes, crafts, gifts.
- Other: the developer (builds and monitors the pipeline); a small-business owner or reviewer who judges label usefulness.

## User discovery questions

1. How do you currently prepare product photos for online listings?
2. Do you resize or rename images manually? How long does a typical batch take?
3. How do you organise and find older product images?
4. Would suggested image labels help? What mistakes would be unacceptable?
5. What would make this service useful enough to adopt?
